# 👋 About Me

I am **SAYEM** — a **Quantitative Finance & Systems Builder** working at the intersection of:

**Quantitative Finance × Algorithmic Trading × Artificial Intelligence × Software Engineering**

I build software-driven systems that transform:

> **Data → Intelligence → Decisions → Execution**

My work focuses on quantitative research, financial modeling, algorithmic trading, AI-powered systems, automation, data infrastructure, and scalable software architecture.

I also build the broader **SAYEMATRIX ecosystem**, turning technical research and ideas into systems, products, ventures, and long-term businesses.

> **I learn, research, build, and turn ideas into systems, products, and businesses.**
